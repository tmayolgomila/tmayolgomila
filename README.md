### 👨‍💻 About me

I build software across every layer of the product: frontend, backend, databases and infrastructure. I focus on solutions designed for **availability, scalability and performance**.

- 🔭 Currently building **Bluewave**, a monitoring and management platform for superyachts at [E-Tech Yachting](https://www.etechyachting.com)
- 🖥️ Responsive **HMI interfaces** in Vue 3 for crew and engineers, plus a **PWA** for onboard use
- ⚙️ Backend services in **Python** for signal processing, alarm management and actuation logic
- 🏭 **PLC integration** via Modbus and OPC UA, and custom industrial middleware built for high availability
- 🗄️ MySQL and InfluxDB management and optimization; Linux servers, Nginx, proxies and SSL
- 👯 Open to collaborating on **naval and industrial software** projects
- 📚 Always learning: recently exploring how AI tools (Claude, Cursor) fit into the daily dev workflow
- 💬 Ask me about **Vue 3, Python, industrial integration, or full-stack architecture**
- 🌐 More of my projects at [tomeumayol.online](https://tomeumayol.online)
